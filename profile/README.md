# Wisdom New Media

**Bringing ideas to life through software, AI, and creative content.**

Based in Malaysia, we work across software development, artificial intelligence,
advertising, and video and short-form drama production.

## What We Do

- **Software Development** — Build applications, digital products, and practical tools.
- **AI Applications** — Explore AI for business workflows and content creation.
- **Advertising & Creative Content** — Combine creativity and technology to tell brand stories.
- **Video & Short-Form Drama** — Develop video content, short dramas, and supporting production tools.

## On GitHub

This is where we share our software projects, developer tools, and technical explorations.

Our first projects are in development. Follow us for updates.
